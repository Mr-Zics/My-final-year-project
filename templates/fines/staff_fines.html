About Fine Records vs. Overdue Books:

Fine Records (This Ledger): Official debts assessed when overdue books are received back at the desk. Fines are charged at ₦50 per overdue day and are payable at the circulation desk.

Overdue Books: Books currently on loan past return deadlines. Fines on those books are accruing estimates and only post to this ledger upon return. View active delinquencies in Overdue Books →
