Circulation Desk & Policies

Key guidelines, physical collection hours, and borrowing rules.

Hours & Location

  • Monday – Friday 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • Saturday 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Sunday & Holidays Closed
Location: Ground Floor, Wing B
Librarians: Emmanuel Udoh & Funke Adeyemi

48-Hour Pickup Hold

When a reserved book is returned by another student, the system automatically marks it Ready and holds the physical copy at the circulation desk.

Collection Deadline: Exactly 48 hours from the timestamp of return.
If uncollected, hold expires and passes to next student in queue.

Circulation Rules

  • Loan Period 7 Days
  • Maximum Borrowed 3 Books
  • Overdue Rate ₦100 / Day
  • Borrowing Block At ₦500 Unpaid
Fines are paid in full at the circulation desk.
